Choose What Suits You

Swedish vs. Aroma vs. Deep Tissue

Not sure which therapy fits your body best today? Here is how to pick your ideal treatment at Bliss Spa BTM Layout.

Swedish Massage

A gentle-to-moderate full-body massage designed for relaxation and relieving muscle tension. Compared with deep tissue, Swedish is gentler and more calming.

Best for: First-timers, light stress, general winding down.

Aroma Massage

Combines gentle-to-moderate massage with inhaled essential oils. Focuses heavily on the sensory aroma experience, calming the nervous system and restoring sleep.

Best for: Sensory calm, sleep improvement, anxiety relief.

Deep Tissue Massage

Uses slow, firm pressure to work deeper into muscles and connective tissues. Target areas include back, shoulders, neck, and legs for stubborn knots.

Best for: Desk stiffness, athletic soreness, intense knots.
